Kareway Health — Patient AAC App
================================
Feature Reference

Bedside communication tool for patients who can't speak comfortably:
they tap a phrase tile and the device speaks it aloud (Tamil or
English), and every interaction is logged so the care team can review
what was said and when.

Six views switch from the top-right nav:
  Board · Trackers · Diet · Meds · History · Settings


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE BOARD  (the main screen)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Phrase tiles
    Tap a tile → the app speaks the phrase aloud and logs the tap.
    Each tile shows an icon, Tamil text on top, English below.
    Tile color encodes its category (red = medical, blue = comfort,
    amber = needs, etc.).

Categories filter
    Pills above the grid: All · Medical · Comfort · Needs · Movement ·
    Social · Emergency. Tap to narrow the grid.

Search
    Type any word — Tamil or English. Press  /  on the keyboard to
    jump straight to the search box. Esc to clear.

Pain scale
    A 1-10 strip lets the patient report pain intensity. The spoken
    phrase mentions the level, and the log captures it.

Emergency button
    Big red button (and a floating "Help" button on phones).
    Speaks the emergency phrase, logs it, and shows a confirmation
    toast that the on-call nurse is on the way.

Family call panel
    Shows the patient's care contacts and one-tap "video call" stubs
    (the actual call integration is left for you to wire up).

Suggestions strip
    Quick tiles based on time-of-day greetings ("Good morning" etc.)
    above the main grid.

Usage today
    Counters in the header show how many phrases were tapped today
    and the average acknowledgment time.

Board summary cards
    Below the phrase grid, four quick-glance cards are shown:
      • Tracker Summary   — today's water / food / restroom / sleep
                           progress vs. goals (links to Trackers view).
      • Diet Summary      — today's macro totals and goal bars
                           (links to Diet view).
      • Medication Reminders — due / overdue doses for the day
                           with one-tap "mark taken" (links to Meds view).
      • Vital Signs       — log and review temperature, BP, SpO₂,
                           pulse, weight, blood glucose inline.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SPEAKING DOCK  (appears when a tile is tapped)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A floating panel at the bottom of the screen with the phrase, a
"Speak" button, and a close button. Lets the patient repeat the phrase
to a nurse who didn't hear it the first time.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TRACKERS VIEW  (nurse-facing daily panel)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Four tracker cards, each with quick-add / quick-subtract buttons,
a daily target, and a colored border (green = met, red = below target):

  Water intake
      Track glasses consumed. 1 glass = 250 ml. Quick-add: +1, +2, +4
      glasses. Resets are per-day per-patient.

  Food & Nutrition
      Separate counters for: Meals eaten, Protein (g), Carbohydrates (g),
      Vitamin / supplement doses. All adjustable in small increments.

  Restroom
      Restroom visit count and urine output in ml (auto-converts to
      litres).

  Sleep
      Total sleep duration in hours. Quick-add in 30-minute steps
      up to +4 h; matching decrements.

  Daily minimum targets
      A full-width "Goals" card at the top lets nursing staff set the
      minimum value for each metric. Values below the minimum are
      shown with a red border and "Below target" badge. Set a goal
      to 0 to disable tracking for that metric.

  All tracker data is stored per-patient and per-day. The last 30 days
  are kept; older data is pruned automatically.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DIET VIEW  (weekly meal log)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A week-at-a-glance view with day columns (Mon–Sun). Navigate back and
forward by week. Each day expands into four meal sections:
  Breakfast · Lunch · Dinner · Snacks

Per food entry you can record:
    Food name, portion size, calories, protein (g), carbs (g),
    fat (g), fiber (g), and free-text notes.

Macro summary
    Running totals (calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber) are shown
    for each meal and for the full day.

Daily goals
    Set calorie and macro targets; progress bars show how close today's
    intake is to the goal. Edit goals inline at the top of the view.

Data is stored per-patient. Entries can be deleted individually.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
MEDICATIONS VIEW  (medication list & daily log)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Maintain a per-patient medication schedule. Each medication record has:
    Name · Generic name · Form (tablet / capsule / liquid / injection /
    inhaler / patch / drops / other) · Dosage · Frequency (once daily,
    twice daily, three times, four times, weekly, alternate days, as
    needed) · Timings (morning / afternoon / evening / night / as
    needed) · Take with food flag · Special instructions · Start & end
    dates · Prescribing doctor · Color tag (for visual identification).

Daily log
    Each scheduled dose appears in the today panel with a check-box.
    Mark doses as taken; the check persists for that calendar day.
    Overdue doses (past scheduled time, not yet marked) are flagged.

The Medication Reminder card on the Board view shows an at-a-glance
summary of today's due and overdue doses without leaving the main
screen.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
VITAL SIGNS  (inline panel on the Board)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Log and review six vital sign readings directly from the Board view.
Each reading is time-stamped. Readings outside the normal range are
highlighted in amber (borderline) or red (critical).

  Temperature   °C  — normal 36.1 – 37.5 °C
  Blood Pressure    — systolic / diastolic mmHg  (e.g. 120/80)
  SpO₂          %   — normal 94 – 100 %
  Pulse         bpm — normal 60 – 100 bpm
  Weight        kg
  Blood glucose mmol/L or mg/dL

All readings are stored per-patient per-day. Previous readings for
the day are shown in a compact list below the entry form.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HISTORY VIEW
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A scrollable timeline of every tap, in reverse-chronological order:
phrase, category, timestamp, and which patient made the tap. Useful
for shift handovers and care reviews. Filtered to the active patient
automatically.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SETTINGS VIEW
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Patient profile
    Name, room, MRN, language preference, voice/speaking-rate,
    care-team contacts.

Phrase editor
    Add, edit, delete, reorder, or reset to defaults. Each phrase
    has: Tamil text, English text, audio text (what's actually
    spoken), icon, category, and color. Drag-and-drop to reorder.

Language
    Toggle the entire UI between Tamil and English.

Accessibility
    - High-contrast / night mode (dark background, high contrast).
    - Dwell-to-select (hover for X ms to activate, instead of tap).
    - Auto-scan mode + scan interval (see below).
    - Haptic feedback toggle (vibrates on tap).
    - Large-text toggle.

Emergency settings
    Confirm-with-hold (require holding the emergency button for
    Xms to prevent accidental triggers).

Data tools
    Reset phrases to defaults, export logs, etc.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
NIGHT MODE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A sun/moon toggle button in the top-right header switches between day
mode (light background) and night mode (dark background, reduced glare).
Preference is saved per-device and restored on the next launch.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SCAN MODE  (single-switch accessibility)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
For patients who can't tap precisely. The board auto-highlights
each tile in turn (interval configurable in Settings). The patient
selects the highlighted tile by pressing the spacebar (or any single
switch mapped to space). A blue banner shows "X / Y" position.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
MULTI-PATIENT
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The patient picker (top-right) shows all patient profiles. Each
patient has their own:
    - Profile data
    - Phrase board layout (their custom phrases / order)
    - Interaction logs
    - Tracker data (water, food, restroom, sleep) and goals
    - Diet log entries and diet goals
    - Medication list and daily dose log
    - Vital signs readings

Switching patients is instant — no reload. Add new patients via the
"+" in the picker, or manage them all from the Patient Manager modal.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
OFFLINE / SYNC
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A small pill in the header shows backend status:
    Live · Speaking enabled    (green)  — backend reachable, all good
    Saving                     (amber)  — write in progress
    Offline                    (red)    — backend not reachable

When offline, the app keeps working from a localStorage cache.
Edits are saved locally and pushed to the backend automatically
when it comes back. Phrases, patients, and logs all survive
restarts and disconnections.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    /          Focus the search box (when on the Board view)
    Space      Select the currently-highlighted tile (in scan mode)
    Esc        Close any open dialog or speaking dock
    Ctrl+C     (in launcher console) Stop the app


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PICTOGRAMS  (ARASAAC)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
The phrase editor can pull images from the free ARASAAC pictogram
library. Type a search term and pick a picture for the tile. Images
are loaded from ARASAAC's CDN, so an internet connection is needed
the first time you pick one (cached after that).


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DATA & PRIVACY
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Everything stays on this computer. The backend writes JSON files
to  project/server/data/  — patients, phrases, layouts, logs. There
is no telemetry and no cloud account by default. (See SETUP.txt for
the optional Firebase mode if you want cloud sync.)

Tracker, diet, medication, and vital signs data are stored in the
browser's localStorage (not the JSON files), so they persist across
restarts as long as the same browser profile is used.


─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TYPICAL PATIENT WORKFLOW
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Nurse opens the app, picks the patient from the picker.
2. Patient taps "I am in pain" → device speaks it, log entry created.
3. Patient picks pain level 7 on the pain scale → "My pain is 7/10"
   spoken and logged.
4. Nurse logs vitals (temperature, BP, SpO₂) on the Board.
5. Nurse logs medications taken for the day in the Meds view or via
   the Medication Reminder card on the Board.
6. Nurse records water intake and meals in the Trackers / Diet views.
7. Nurse reviews History view at shift change to see the day's taps.
8. Nurse adjusts phrases in Settings to match this patient's needs.

That's the whole loop. Everything else (multi-patient, scan mode,
high contrast / night mode, etc.) layers onto that core flow.
